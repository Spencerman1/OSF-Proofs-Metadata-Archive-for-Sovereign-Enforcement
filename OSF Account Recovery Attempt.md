### **Overall frame**

**Objective:**  
 Document, in a reproducible and time‑bounded way, that you:

* attempted in good faith to reactivate the OSF account  
* followed all visible and reasonable channels  
* recorded every interaction and system response  
* preserved all artifacts in a vault‑ready structure

**Window:**  
 5 days, with three escalating tiers of contact and explanation.

---

### **Tier 1 — Direct system interaction (Day 1–2)**

**Goal:** Exhaust all self‑service and visible OSF mechanisms.

* **Actions:**  
  * **Login attempts:** normal login, password reset, alternate login methods (SSO, ORCID, etc.).  
  * **Recovery flows:** “Forgot password”, “Need help logging in”, any visible recovery links.  
  * **Direct URL access:** old project links, DOIs, public pages, profile URL.  
* **Recording:**  
  * Full‑screen video of each attempt.  
  * Verbal narration: date, time, what you’re doing, what you expect, what actually happens.  
* **Artifacts to vault:**  
  * Screen recordings  
  * Screenshots of errors  
  * Any emails received (or not received) from OSF

---

### **Tier 2 — Formal support contact (Day 2–3)**

**Goal:** Demonstrate that you escalated through official human channels.

* **Actions:**  
  * Use OSF’s support/contact form (on camera).  
  * Send a **clear, concise email** to support (and any listed admin contact).  
  * If available: open a ticket through any institutional or helpdesk portal.  
* **Content of message:**  
  * Identify the account (email, username, approximate creation date).  
  * State that the account previously existed and is now inaccessible.  
  * Request reactivation or export of data.  
  * Ask for confirmation that the account exists in their system.  
* **Recording:**  
  * Screen recording of composing and submitting forms/emails.  
  * Save sent emails as PDFs.  
* **Artifacts to vault:**  
  * Copies of all messages sent  
  * Auto‑replies or ticket numbers  
  * Any human responses

---

### **Tier 3 — Clarifying explanation & final escalation (Day 4–5)**

**Goal:** Create a final, unambiguous record that you:

* explained the situation clearly  
* requested specific actions  
* gave them a reasonable response window

**Actions:**

* **Follow‑up email** referencing:  
  * prior attempts (with dates)  
  * lack of resolution or response  
  * the importance of the account and its contents  
* **Clarify requests:**  
  * Confirm whether the account exists and its status (active, disabled, deleted).  
  * Request either:  
    * reactivation, or  
    * export of all associated data, or  
    * written confirmation that the account and its contents are no longer recoverable.  
* **Recording:**  
  * Screen recording of drafting and sending the follow‑up.  
  * Optional: short video log summarizing the 5‑day sequence.

**Artifacts to vault:**

* Final email(s)  
* Any responses or lack thereof (screenshots of empty inbox at key checkpoints)  
* A short written summary of the 3‑tier process and outcomes
